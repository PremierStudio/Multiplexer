# Third-party icons

Brand marks used in the Integrations and MCP lists come from
[Dashboard Icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons)
(Apache 2.0, Homarr Labs). Product names and trademarks remain their
owners'. Icons are identification only.

Chrome glyphs (chat, cores, terminal, …) are first-party text marks,
not Dashboard Icons assets.
